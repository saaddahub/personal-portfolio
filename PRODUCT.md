# Saad Akhtar — portfolio

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Purpose
Saad Akhtar's personal portfolio at saadsalam.dev. A founder, recruiter, lecturer or collaborator opens a link, understands within seconds who Saad is (full-stack developer, BS Artificial Intelligence student at UMT, Lahore), browses selected work, and gets in touch by email.

## Audience
People deciding whether to work with Saad: founders hiring a full-stack developer, internship and research leads, and peers. Most arrive from a shared link on a laptop or phone, often in the evening, with a minute to spare.

## Constraints
Single-page site. Vite + React 18, plain CSS, Lenis smooth scrolling and one canvas engine for the signature scene. No CMS or admin; content lives in `src/content.js`. Must run at 60fps on mid-range phones, honour `prefers-reduced-motion` and `prefers-reduced-transparency`, and work fully by keyboard. Deploys as a static build on Vercel.

## Evidence
Projects, education, experience and links come from Saad's own previous portfolio data: Netflix Data Visualiser (live), Hotel Management System, Hospital Database, AI Interviewer. No invented clients, metrics or testimonials. The LinkedIn URL is unconfirmed.

## Brand commitments
The user pinned the world: a pink blossom tree at night whose petals are carried away by the wind, minimalist and modern, with Apple-smooth motion. Inspiration reference: the v0 "COMPUTE" template (dark ground, glowing pink tree, drifting particles); the build takes its feeling, never its assets or layout.

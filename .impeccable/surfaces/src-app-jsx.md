## Direction contract

THESIS: A cherry tree at night is the page's ground, and its wind is the visitor's hand. It refuses the category default of a split hero with a picture on one side and a card grid of projects below.

OWN-WORLD: Plum-black night (#0d0a10), mist text (#f4ede9), blossom pinks (#f5a9c2, #d9577e, #ffdbe6) owned by the tree and petals, a drenched dawn field (#f3c6d3) to close. One thin variable grotesque (Bricolage Grotesque) at every size, no monospace, no labels above headings, lucide line icons, one translucent pill nav.

STORY: The visitor sees the tree grow, learns Saad's name and craft beneath it, reads one sentence about how he works, opens real projects in a typographic index, meets him in About, and writes to him in the dawn field where the petals land.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed canvas tree, trunk from the lower right, canopy across the top of the screen. Name "Saad Akhtar" at the lower left, thin, up to 6rem. Below it the role (full-stack developer and AI student in Lahore) followed by his one-line pitch from his own copy ("Useful interfaces, thoughtful systems, built with curiosity."). Actions "See the work" (primary) and "Get in touch". Wordmark and three links in the nav. A small hint invites the visitor to stir the wind: lower right on desktop, under the actions on phones. The tree canvas lags the scroll so the canopy lingers past the first viewport, and off-tree petals keep crossing every section.

FORM: Pinned by the user (blossom tree, wind-carried petals, dark, minimalist, Apple-smooth motion). concept-seed not run because a user-pinned direction beats the roll. Build path: code-led. Signature interaction: pointer and scroll velocity become wind in a particle field; petals settle on the closing ground. Motion grammar: exponential ease-out reveals from a visible default, scroll-scrubbed statement, press feedback on pointer-down.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
